About the Fund

Capital for operating businesses, not real estate.

Fenelon Opportunity Fund Inc. was formed to back business ventures directly — the founders and operators building companies, not passive real estate or tax-advantaged investment programs. We are not a Qualified Opportunity Fund and do not invest in Opportunity Zones.

The office is run directly by its founder, who is personally accountable for every commitment on the book. There is no committee to dilute conviction — every position is one we would be glad to hold for a generation.

How We Work

Three disciplines, applied in order.

Simple by design — simplicity is what makes it repeatable across cycles, and across generations.

Source

Go direct, go deep

Opportunities come from relationships and primary diligence, not brokers or auctions. We look for businesses and assets undervalued because of complexity, neglect, or timing — never because the underlying quality is broken.

Underwrite

Size the margin of safety

Every commitment is underwritten to a specific view of intrinsic value and a specific reason it's mispriced today. If we can't state both plainly, we don't make the investment.

Hold

Let the thesis play out

Commitments are sized to be meaningful and held with a multi-year, often multi-generational horizon. We engage directly and constructively wherever it serves the position.
