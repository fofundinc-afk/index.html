About the Fund

A small, disciplined mandate — not a product line.

Fenelon Opportunity Fund Inc. was formed to do one thing well: steward the family's capital across a small number of opportunities we understand deeply, without the constraints or reporting demands of outside capital.

The office is managed directly by its founder, who is personally accountable for every commitment on the book. There is no committee diluting conviction and no style drift chasing quarterly flows — only a small number of holdings, sized to reflect how strongly we believe in them.

How We Work

Three disciplines, applied in order.

The process is simple by design. Simplicity is what makes it repeatable across cycles and across generations.

01 — Source

Go direct, go deep

Opportunities come from relationships and primary diligence, not brokers or auctions. We look for businesses and assets that are undervalued because of complexity, neglect, or timing — not because the underlying quality is broken.

02 — Underwrite

Size the margin of safety

Every commitment is underwritten to a specific view of intrinsic value and a specific reason it's mispriced today. If we can't articulate both in plain language, we don't make the investment.

03 — Hold

Let the thesis play out

Commitments are sized to be meaningful and held with a multi-year, often multi-generational horizon. We engage directly and constructively wherever it serves the position.

Where We Look

Situations, not sectors.

We don't organize the portfolio by industry. We organize it by the kind of mispricing we're exploiting.

A Overlooked Compounders Durable, well-run businesses passed over because they're too small or too quiet for institutional attention.
B Special Situations Restructurings, successions, and corporate transitions that create temporary, correctable mispricings.
C Real Assets & Property Real estate and other tangible assets acquired directly, on our own timeline, without fund-cycle pressure.
D Owner-Operator Alignment Partnerships with founders and operators who think and act like long-term owners.
